# Privacy Policy

## What Is Collected
Notes2Flow does not collect any personal information. No emails, no accounts.

## What Happens to Your Notes
- Your notes are sent to **Groq API** for AI processing
- For details on how Groq handles your data, see their [privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy)

## Data Security
By using notes2flow, acknowledge:
- Your notes are transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit)
- Groq API has their own data handling practices
- For details, see [Groq's privacy policy](https://groq.com/privacy)

## What You Shouldn't Use This For

Don't use notes2flow for:
- Passwords or credentials
- Medical or health information
- Financial data
- Confidential business information
- Any other sensitive/personal information

Since notes are sent to external APIs, treat them like you're sharing with a third party.

## Third-Party Services
- **Groq API** — Processes your notes ([Groq Privacy Policy](https://groq.com/privacy))
- **Netlify** — Hosts our frontend ([Netlify Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/))
- **Render** — Hosts our backend ([Render Privacy Policy](https://render.com/privacy))
